Review management

Turn completed jobs into honest Google reviews

We help local businesses ask every customer for a review and answer new reviews quickly, with you approving everything that posts.

1

We ask your customers

We email a review request to your customers. Every customer is asked the same way, with the same link, whether or not they were happy. We follow FTC guidance and never filter or “gate” who gets asked.

2

Customers review you on Google

Your customers write their own honest reviews on your Google profile. We never write reviews for them or offer anything in exchange.

3

We draft replies, you approve

We draft replies to new reviews. You approve what posts, and nothing is posted automatically.

4

You watch it grow over time

A monthly scorecard tracks new reviews and replies. Results depend on your customers, so we can’t promise a review count, star rating or ranking.

What you can count on

Two things we do for every voice receptionist client, in plain terms.

Tested before it goes live

Before we turn on your receptionist, we test your urgent-call path with your team. That includes a live transfer to each of your two on-call phones and one deliberately failed transfer, to confirm the urgent email and voice page reach you. You sign off on the test. If an emergency scenario fails, we fix it and test again before going live.

Usage alerts and a published overage rate

Your plan includes 500 billed minutes a month. We email your billing contacts at 375, 450 and 500 minutes (75%, 90% and 100%). Above 500 minutes, usage is $0.30 a minute, counted by the second and charged to your card on file. Your monthly statement shows what was used.

The receptionist is an AI assistant. Call transfers, pages and alerts depend on phone carriers and other outside services, so we can't promise any single call is answered or any alert is received.
