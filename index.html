About RoMar Digital

A digital agency based in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, focused on practical results for local and growing businesses.

Who we are

RoMar Digital was founded by experienced industry veterans who saw too many businesses struggle with fragmented digital tools and slow response times. We build and operate systems that capture leads, answer customers, and protect reputation — without adding headcount.

What we focus on

Our work centers on two high-impact areas right now: AI voice agents that answer missed calls for service businesses (starting with HVAC), and review generation and management that keeps Google profiles strong and trustworthy.

How we work

We start with a clear pilot. Setup is straightforward, pricing is transparent, and we measure results in calls answered, jobs booked, and reviews gained. Ongoing service includes monitoring, overage alerts, and direct support.
